# Privacy Policy for Chatmask

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Chatmask is a Chrome extension that hides sidebar conversation titles on ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini by replacing them with gibberish on your device.

## Data we collect

Chatmask does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share personal information. There is no account, no analytics, and no backend.

## What stays on your device

The extension uses Chrome’s local `storage` API only to remember:

- whether masking is on or off
- which apps you selected (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini)

That data never leaves your browser.

## What we do not access

Chatmask does not read message bodies, prompts, files, cookies, passwords, or payment information. It only changes sidebar Recents / history titles on the sites listed above, and only when you turn masking on.

## Hosts

The extension runs only on ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini pages so it can mask those sidebars. It does not run on other websites.

## Remote code

Chatmask does not load scripts from the internet. All code ships inside the extension package.
